# 资产参考图提示词模板（《越线》资产图写法 v2）

`tools/asset_gaps.py` 会用这些模板 + library 里的描述，自动给缺图的资产写好提示词。这里是原文，手写时复制。
挑图：最可信的脸、眼睛有细小眼神光；场景只有一个光源方向；可变物体是动作之前的状态。

## 公共前缀
```
Create a clean production reference image sheet for the live-action short drama "<剧名>". Use premium grounded live-action realism, natural skin and fabric texture with visible pores, physically plausible materials, and a neutral professional art-department presentation. This is a static reference sheet, not a video frame: no motion blur, no dialogue, no subtitles, no story action, no camera movement.

Preserve the exact identity, age, proportions, wardrobe logic, spatial topology, material and state described below. Do not invent a logo, brand, readable text, readable number, signature, watermark or graphic interface. Leave documents, screens, labels and signs blank for post-production.
```

## 角色：三栏表（左面部特写、中正面全身去头、右背面全身）
```
<公共前缀>

Layout (three panels side by side on a flat neutral mid-grey studio backdrop with soft even light): LEFT, a large three-quarter close-up portrait of the face and shoulders, the face turned slightly to one side, eyes with small natural catchlights; MIDDLE, a full-body front view with the head cropped out of frame at the top edge so only the neck down shows; RIGHT, a full-body back view. Same real person and identical wardrobe in all three panels. Plain and unstyled: no cinematic lighting, no colour grade.

Character: <名字>, <年龄、脸、发型、体型>. Wardrobe: <参考图上要出现的服装>. All characters are adults unless stated otherwise.

Negative constraints: no readable text, no random letters or numbers, no logos, no watermark, no subtitles, no duplicated limbs, no extra fingers, no mirrored geography, no identity drift, no age drift, no costume change, no glamour fashion styling, no film grain or cinematic colour grade on character sheets, no unexplained prop, no extra people.
```

## 局部修图（派生状态：换装、湿透、受伤）
```
Edit the attached reference image: <一句话目标>. CHANGE: <只写唯一要变的>. PRESERVE EXACTLY: the face, identity, age, hair, body proportions, pose, panel layout, camera angle, background, lighting and every detail not named in CHANGE. ONLY CHANGE: <只写唯一要变的>. 100% identical otherwise.
```
"改过头了"＝CHANGE 写太多、PRESERVE 写太少。一张图不要整图重绘两次。

## 镜头里常用的状态追踪句（RH 验证：E43 基线 2/2 提前出场，加句后 2/2 干净）
```
the chamber door stays shut and sealed from [Shot 1] until it hisses open in [Shot 4]; Cade is inside the sealed chamber, out of sight, until he steps out in [Shot 5].
```
并在被参考图带偏的那一镜里重复写（"stands with its door shut and sealed"）。
